/methodology: cost modeler

Our calculations assume average prompt context of 1,200 tokens and 300 completion tokens per business action.

  • Proprietary baseline: Direct calls to commercial frontier APIs at ~$20–$30 / 1M output tokens without caching.
  • Minsky model-routed stack: 70-80% of classification, extraction, and drafting routed to quantized open-source weights (Llama 3.3 / Mistral) on dedicated inference instances, utilizing Redis semantic vector caching, cascading to frontier models only when uncertainty thresholds exceed 15%.
